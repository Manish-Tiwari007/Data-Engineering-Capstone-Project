2.  Technical  Architecture  &  Standards  A.  Snowflake  Environment  &  Layering  You  must  implement  a  three-layer  Medallion  Architecture  within  Snowflake  to  ensure  data  
integrity
 
and
 
traceability.
 
●  Bronze  (RAW):  Ingest  raw,  immutable  dummy  data.  Use  a  landing  zone  approach.  ●  Silver  (STAGING):  Perform  cleaning,  schema  enforcement,  casting,  and  deduplication.  ●  Gold  (PROD):  Final  Star  Schema  (Facts  and  Dimensions)  optimized  for  analytics.  ●  Naming  Convention:  Database  must  be  named  MBUST_{{student_roll}}.  
B.  Data  Modeling  &  SCD  Type  2  ●  Dimensional  Modeling:  Your  Gold  layer  must  consist  of  at  least  one  Fact  table  and  
three
 
Dimension
 
tables
.
 ●  Historical  Tracking:  Implement  Slowly  Changing  Dimensions  (SCD  Type  2)  for  at  
least
 
one
 
core
 
dimension

historical
 
changes

 ●  Calendar:  Use  an  existing  Date/Calendar  dimension  from  the  Snowflake  Marketplace  
(building
 
your
 
own
 
is
 
optional).
 
C.  Orchestration  &  Engineering  (Airflow)  ●  Modular  DAGs:  Break  down  your  pipeline  into  logical,  decoupled  tasks  (Extract  →  Load  
→
 
Stage
 
→
 
Transform).
 ●  Idempotency:  The  pipeline  must  be  re-runnable  for  the  same  period  without  creating

 ●  XComs  &  Hooks:  Use  standard  Airflow  Providers  and  manage  credentials  via  Airflow
